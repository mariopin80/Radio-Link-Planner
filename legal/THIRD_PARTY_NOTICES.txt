RADIO LINK PLANNER — THIRD-PARTY COMPONENTS AND DATA
Working inventory for the next release; revision 2026-11-18.

This notice identifies known third-party components and points to their
licences. It is NOT YET a complete inventory of transitive Python/native
dependencies. Complete the release-package audit before commercial launch.

1. NTIA ITS Irregular Terrain Model (ITM), upstream NTIA/itm.
   https://github.com/NTIA/itm
   Licence and modification history are distributed alongside this file as
   LICENSE.md and NOTICE.md. NTIA does not endorse Radio Link Planner.

2. Py1812, by Ivica Stevanovic (eeveetza), pinned upstream implementation.
   https://github.com/eeveetza/Py1812
   See Py1812-NOTICE.txt for the author's permissions and the documented
   changes to selected adapted numerical routines.

3. Py2001, by Ivica Stevanovic (eeveetza), pinned upstream implementation.
   https://github.com/eeveetza/Py2001
   See Py2001-NOTICE.txt for the upstream permission and acknowledgement.

4. MapLibre GL JS 5.24.0, BSD 3-Clause and embedded-component notices.
   https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js
   Its full upstream LICENSE.txt accompanies the included map assets.

5. Copernicus WorldDEM-30 (GLO-30) digital elevation data.
   https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM
   Data may be downloaded by the individual user. The application must
   provide the source credit and no-liability notice prescribed by the
   Copernicus licence whenever data or derived results are communicated.
   The official wording is embedded in radiolink/legal_notices.py and
   included in Copernicus-based HTML, Markdown and PDF reports.

   Required attribution for adapted GLO-30 data:
   produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and
   © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS
   by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved

   Required disclaimer where Copernicus WorldDEM-30 data are distributed:
   The organisations in charge of the Copernicus programme by law or by
   delegation do not incur any liability for any use of the Copernicus
   WorldDEM-30.

6. OpenStreetMap: map data licensed under ODbL.
   https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
   Rendered tiles currently come from https://tile.openstreetmap.org
   under a SEPARATE tile-use policy; attribution is shown on each map.
   https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/
   No OpenStreetMap data or tile archive is bundled in the installer.

7. Python dependencies (including NumPy, Pydantic, ReportLab, Matplotlib,
   Rasterio/GDAL/PROJ, pywebview and their transitive dependencies) are
   governed by their individual licences. PyInstaller's bundling exception
   permits proprietary applications; this does NOT override dependencies'
   licences. A version-resolved SBOM and full binary-package licence review
   remain necessary before the paid release.

8. Inno Setup is used to create the Windows installer. The developer asks
   commercial users to acquire a commercial licence, while stating that
   purchase is not strictly required; review current upstream terms.
   https://jrsoftware.org/inno-setup/purchase

ITU-R integral digital products are intentionally excluded from the
distributable. The user downloads them from official ITU sources into the
user's local profile. Commercial automated-download terms remain subject to
formal clarification with the ITU; see legal/ITU-CLARIFICATION-REQUEST.en.md.

Radio Link Planner's own proprietary licence is separate from every licence
above. No preliminary licence draft changes third-party rights.
